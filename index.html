Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about admissions, schedules, and daily care.

What are the school hours and day care options? expand_more
Our regular preschool sessions run from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM, Monday through Friday. We also provide safe extended day care and after-school enrichment options until 5:00 PM for working parents.
Is GPS-tracked school transportation available? expand_more
Yes, we operate safe and air-conditioned transport vans equipped with real-time GPS tracking, speed regulators, and female attendants to supervise children door-to-door within a 10km radius.
What is the student-to-teacher ratio in classes? expand_more
We maintain an intimate 1:8 ratio for our toddler playgroups and 1:10 for nursery and kindergarten classes. This guarantees personalized attention, emotional warmth, and individual pace matching.
Are meals and hygiene protocols provided? expand_more
Yes, we serve fresh, organic mid-morning snacks prepared daily in our certified hygienic kitchen. All play spaces, toys, and restrooms undergo deep sanitization twice daily.
Our Story & Philosophy

Nurturing Curiosity,
Inspiring Joy

At Kanha Kids, we believe early childhood is a magical time of discovery. Our mission is to provide a safe, warm, and playful environment where every child feels empowered to explore and grow at their own unique pace.

How We Started

A Place That Feels Like Home

Kanha Kids began with a simple yet profound vision: to create a space that felt like an extension of a loving home. Founded by experienced educators, we noticed a critical need for an early education space that prioritized emotional well-being alongside cognitive development.

What started as a small playgroup has blossomed into a vibrant, trusted community of hundreds of families. We continuously innovate our practices by blending international early childhood standards with deep empathy for each child's innate curiosity.
